01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The real world doesn’t
come with an API.

Local knowledge is scattered everywhere.

An event changes venue. A retailer has a product in stock. A road closes. A business opens.

Important updates appear on social media, local radio and business websites, but never reach the systems people rely on. Traditional search and AI frequently miss these signals.

VeriSitu is working to bridge that gap.

From fragmented information
to connected intelligence.

We’re developing infrastructure to transform fragmented local data into actionable intelligence, helping people, businesses and AI systems better understand the world around them.

  1. 01 ↗

    Discover

    Find relevant information across diverse local sources.

  2. 02 ↗

    Understand

    Use AI to extract meaning, entities and relationships.

  3. 03 ↗

    Connect

    Reconcile information, preserve its provenance and understand what has changed.

  4. 04 ↗

    Deliver

    Make intelligence accessible through applications, search and APIs.

The aim: reusable infrastructure for the next generation of AI-powered services.
